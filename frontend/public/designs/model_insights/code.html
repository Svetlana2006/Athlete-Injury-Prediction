ROC-AUC vs PR-AUC in Injury Forecasting

The Imbalanced Metric Paradox

Standard classification accuracy is mathematically deceptive in professional sports analytics: when over 98.8% of daily athlete sessions conclude without tissue trauma, a naive model predicting "zero risk every day" achieves an impressive 98.8% accuracy while failing 100% of real clinical interventions.

While ROC-AUC measures rank separation between healthy and susceptible sessions, PR-AUC evaluates true alarm utility by directly isolating positive precision on rare acute-injury clusters.

False Pos Rate Floor: 0.04% Lift Over Random: 3.4x

Focal Loss Formulation

Class Imbalance Regularization
FL(p_t) = -α_t · (1 - p_t)^γ · log(p_t)

In standard cross-entropy loss, cumulative gradients from tens of thousands of uncomplicated training days drown out critical signals from rare tendon strains.

The modulating factor (1 - p_t)^γ dynamically penalizes well-classified negative instances (where p_t → 1), forcing the backpropagation optimizer to allocate attention to ambiguous, high-risk microtrauma signals.

Downweight Factor: γ = 2.0 Weight: α = 0.25
