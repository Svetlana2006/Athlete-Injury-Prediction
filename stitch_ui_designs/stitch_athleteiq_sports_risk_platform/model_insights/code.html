What ROC-AUC Measures

Area Under the Receiver Operating Characteristic (ROC-AUC) quantifies the probability that the scoring algorithm assigns a higher risk estimate to a randomly chosen positive case (an athlete who sustains an acute injury within 3 days) than to a randomly chosen negative case (an uninjured training day).

check Scale: 0.5 (Random Guessing) to 1.0 (Optimal Discriminator).
METRIC_SPEC_02 area_chart

What PR-AUC Measures

The Precision-Recall Area calculates the area under the curve formed by Precision (True Positives / Predicted Positives) plotted against Recall (Sensitivity). Unlike ROC, it isolates performance purely on the minority class without inflation from thousands of straightforward true negatives.

warning_amber Gold standard evaluation for rare event incidence.

Why PR-AUC Matters for Imbalanced Sports Data

In professional athletics, genuine injuries are extremely sparse—uninjured training sessions typically outnumber injuries 99 to 1. A naive model predicting “No Injury” every day scores 99% accuracy. In this regime, standard ROC curves can stay above 0.90 even when alerts generate an unusable 15 false alarms per true event.

flaky Prevents coach notification fatigue from false alerts.

Focal Loss Mechanics & Hyperparameter Tuning

FL(p_t) = -α_t · (1 - p_t)^γ · log(p_t)

Standard cross entropy allows easy negatives to dominate gradient updates. Focal Loss introduces a dynamic modulating factor (1 - p_t)^γ with γ = 2.0, dramatically suppressing loss from confident uninjured days. Simultaneously, α = 0.25 scales rare class gradients to prevent backprop collapse.

tune Configured: α=0.25, γ=2.0 (Verified via Grid Search).
